Mini cheesepies recipe

Description:

Easy air fryer mini cheese pies

Ingredients:

  • 300 g feta cheese
  • 150 g gruyere cheese, grated
  • 70 g butter, spreadable
  • 300 g strained yogurt, full-fat
  • 1 tablespoon(s) oregano, dried
  • 1 egg, medium
  • 280 g self-rising flour
  • 1 pinch granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 egg yolk, diluted with 1 tablespoon water
  • 1 tablespoon(s) sesame seeds

Steps:

  1. The air fryer used for this recipe has a rectangular basket with an 8-liter capacity and a power output of 1700 Watts.

  2. Put the butter into a small bowl, cover the bowl with plastic wrap, and microwave it for ½-1 minute at 800 Watts until melted.

  3. Remove the bowl from the microwave and set it aside.

  4. Crumble the feta into big pieces with your hands directly into a large bowl.

  5. Add the gruyere cheese, yogurt, oregano, egg, and melted butter, and mix them with a spoon until completely combined.

  6. Add the flour, sugar, and salt, and mix until fully incorporated into the mixture. (If the cheeses you are using are already quite salty, you can omit the salt.)

  7. Line the air fryer basket with parchment paper and place it next to the bowl.

  8. Wear gloves and mix the mixture again with your hands so that the ingredients bind together even more.

  9. Shape the mixture into 16 balls and place half of them in the basket.

  10. Brush the cheese pies with the egg wash and sprinkle them with sesame seeds.

  11. Place the basket in the air fryer and cook the mini cheese pies at 180°C (350°F) for 18-20 minutes.

  12. Remove the basket from the air fryer, follow the same process for the second batch, and serve.
