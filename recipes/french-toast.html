French toast recipe

Description:

Quick and easy French toast recipe

Ingredients:

  • 200 g whole milk
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 30 gbrown sugar
  • orange zest, of 1 orange
  • 300 gbread of toast
  • seed oil, for frying

Steps:

  1. In a bowl, add the milk, eggs, salt, baking powder, vanilla extract, brown sugar and orange zest. Whisk until all of the ingredients are completely incorporated.

  2. Cut the bread into thick, even slices. Add them to the mixture and allow them to soak a little..

  3. Transfer to a deep pan with a little vegetable oil and cook the slices for 4-5 minutes over medium heat until golden. Add the slices in batches so that they can cook properly.

  4. When ready, transfer to a baking pan lined with paper towels.
