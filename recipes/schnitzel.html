Schnitzel Recipe

Description:

A classic schnitzel recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1kg pork, loin
  • 200 g all-purpose flour
  • 6 eggs
  • 2 tablespoon(s) mustard
  • 1 tablespoon(s) oregano
  • 200 g breadcrumbs
  • 300 ml sunflower oil
  • pepper
  • salt

Steps:

  1. Cut the pork into very thin slices. Pound each slice with a meat mallet or pounder in order to spread each piece and soften it. Ideally, you should cover the pork with plastic wrap if you use a meat pounder.

  2. In a bowl add the flour, salt, pepper, and mix.

  3. In another bowl break the eggs, add salt, pepper, the mustard, the oregano, and mix well.

  4. In a third bowl add the breadcrumbs, salt, pepper, and mix.

  5. Place a deep frying pan with sunflower oil over medium-low heat.

  6. Season each piece of pork with salt and pepper. Dredge each piece into the flour, then the eggs, the breadcrumbs, and add it to the pan. Fry in batches for 1 ½ – 2 minutes on each side until golden brown and cooked through. Follow the same process for all of the pork.

  7. Remove, place on paper towels, and serve with French fries, coleslaw salad, lemon slices, and thyme.
